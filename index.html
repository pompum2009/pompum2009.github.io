Frequently asked questions

What is RELAX?

It's an app with sounds for background, rest and sleep. 100 nature sounds and atmospheres, relaxing audio and meditations — all in one place, with a simple interface and a timer.

Is it only a sleep app?

No. Sleep is just one of the scenarios. The sounds are just as often used for work and concentration, while studying, for meditation, reading or simply resting.

How many sounds are there?

100 sounds: rain, ocean, forest, fire, white noise and many more.

Can I leave the sound playing without switching it off manually?

Yes, there's a timer for that. Pick the time you need and the sound stops by itself.

Is the app really free?

Yes. The app is free and supported by ads.

Which languages is the app available in?

English, Ukrainian, Simplified Chinese and Russian.

Which devices does it work on?

Any Android phone or tablet. Open in Google Play
